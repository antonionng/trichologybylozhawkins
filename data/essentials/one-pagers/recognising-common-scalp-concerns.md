## At the chair

- Lead with what you can see, not a diagnosis.
- Ask: "I've noticed a change here — have you noticed anything?"
- If it is red, spreading, or painful, pause chemical services and guide towards professional assessment.

## Quick visual language (stub)

- Dry white flakes, little redness: describe flakes; suggest a measured over-the-counter conversation.
- Greasy yellow scale on a red base: describe inflammation; pharmacist or GP if it persists.
- Thick silvery plaques with sharp borders: be gentle; GP or trichologist for management.
- Redness that follows product placement: stop the likely product; document; patch-test later services.

## Referral script (stub)

"I can see some change on the scalp that I would rather a specialist look at with you. I am not diagnosing — I just want you to get a clear answer. I can note what I have observed today if that helps your GP or trichologist."

## Do not say

- "You have psoriasis / dermatitis / alopecia."
- "This treatment will fix it."
- "You must see a doctor immediately" unless there is acute distress or an urgent red-flag presentation your salon protocol already covers.
