# [DRAFT PLACEHOLDER] Chair-side: Know Your Boundaries

Marketing: replace this stub with the final scope-of-practice one-pager. Do not treat this as legal advice.

## Within scope

- Observe hair and scalp changes.
- Describe what you see in plain language.
- Recommend over-the-counter care and salon treatments you are trained to give.
- Refer to a trichologist, GP, or dermatologist.
- Follow up at the next visit.

## Outside scope

- Naming a medical diagnosis.
- Recommending prescription medicines.
- Treating a diagnosed medical condition as if you were the clinician.
- Promising that hair will or will not grow back.
- Any procedure that breaks the skin.

## Safe / unsafe language (stub)

- Safe: "I've noticed some scaling here."
- Unsafe: "You have psoriasis."
- Safe: "A specialist could give you a clearer answer."
- Unsafe: "You don't need a doctor — I can treat this."

## If they ask you to treat a red, weeping scalp

"I can see there is irritation, and I do not think it is right to put products on it today. Please see your GP first so we do not make it worse. I am happy to see you again once it is settled."
