## Opening the door

- "I've noticed some changes here — have you noticed too?"
- "Your scalp looks a little different from last time — has anything changed for you?"
- Avoid: "You're losing your hair" / "That doesn't look right."

## If they want a name for it

"That is exactly the kind of question a trichologist or GP can answer properly. What I can do today is describe what I see and help you decide the next step."

## Product and service language (stub)

- Match the concern you can see to an over-the-counter or salon service you are trained to offer.
- Set a time frame: many home-care changes need several weeks, not one wash.
- Do not prescribe. Do not promise a cure.

## When they become emotional

- Acknowledge: "I can see this is upsetting, and that is understandable."
- Offer a quieter moment if you can.
- Do not minimise and do not promise regrowth.

## Referral as support

"A specialist who sees this every day can give you a clearer picture. I will still look after your hair in the salon — this is an extra pair of eyes, not me stepping away."
